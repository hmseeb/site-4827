FAQ

Questions Phoenix customers ask us most

How much does a cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on the size of the space, its condition and how often you want service. Most recurring Phoenix home cleanings land between $120 and $220 per visit, and deep or move-out cleans are quoted higher because they take longer. You always get a flat written price before we start — no hourly surprises.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not at all. Most of our customers are at work. You can leave a key, a lockbox code or a gate code on file, and we will text you when the crew arrives and when they finish.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes. Our cleaners arrive with vacuums, microfiber cloths, mops and all products included in your price. If you prefer we use your own products — for allergies, stone counters or a specific finish — just leave them out and let us know.

Are your products safe for pets and children?

Our standard lineup is low-odor and pet- and kid-safe. We can go fully fragrance-free on request. For disinfecting medical or dental offices we use EPA-registered products and follow the required dwell times.

Is the same cleaner assigned every visit?

For recurring customers, yes — you get an assigned cleaner or small team who learns your home. If they are ever out sick or on vacation, a trained backup follows your written checklist so nothing gets missed.

What if I am not happy with the result?

Tell us within 48 hours and we come back and re-clean the areas in question at no charge. We are licensed, bonded and insured, so you are covered in the rare case something is damaged.

How far in advance should I book?

Recurring slots are usually available within a week. Deep cleans and move-out jobs are best booked 5 to 7 days out, though we often have same-week openings. Call us for urgent requests and we will tell you what is truly available.

What is your cancellation policy?

Reschedule or cancel free up to 24 hours before your appointment. Inside 24 hours we charge a small fee, because that time slot is already reserved for you and paid to your cleaner.
